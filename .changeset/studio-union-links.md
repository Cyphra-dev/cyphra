`cyphra studio` links and unlinks related records: _Link…_ in a related view connects a record by its key (with the edge properties of a `relationship { … }` model), and each row can be unlinked. For a relation to a union, a member picker comes first (a node, or an interface member by the interface's key). Edge properties can be edited from either direction of the followed relation, relationship models to a union list and edit their edges with each end's member key, and union tables show each row's key.
