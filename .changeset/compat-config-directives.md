`compat.v7.makeSchema` supports the v7 schema configuration directives with v7's exact schema and behaviour: `@mutation(operations)`, `@query(read, connection, aggregate)` (type, union and `extend schema` level), `@selectable`, `@settable`, `@filterable`, `@sortable` (fields, relationships and relationship properties, including their effect on nested inputs) and `@limit(default, max)` on every read of the type. Directives and arguments it does not implement are now always rejected with `UnsupportedTypeDefsError` instead of ignored: `@coalesce`, `@unique`, unknown directives on relationship fields, enums and enum values, and extra directives on `@jwt` / `@relationshipProperties` types. A root field hidden by `@query` / `@mutation` can be redefined in `Query` / `Mutation` and served by the application's resolvers (it never gets the generated resolver), and `@selectable` hides fields returning types without `@node`; combining `@customResolver` with a configuration directive is rejected like v7. Deliberate differences from v7: `limit: 0` / `first: 0` no longer bypass `@limit(max)`, and `@selectable(onRead: false)` on a to-one relationship (an invalid schema in v7) is rejected.
