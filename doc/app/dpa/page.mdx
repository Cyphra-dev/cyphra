# Data Processing Agreement

**Last updated: 2026-11-28**

This Data Processing Agreement ("**DPA**") applies when FAKGROUP processes personal data on your
behalf in Cyphra Cloud. It meets the requirements of Article 28 of the EU General Data Protection
Regulation (GDPR) and is part of the [Terms of Service](/terms).

## 1. Parties and acceptance

1.1. The parties are you, the customer, as **controller** ("**Customer**"), and **FAKGROUP**,
`[FAKGROUP legal form]`, `[registered address]`, `[registration number (SIREN/RCS)]`, as
**processor** ("**FAKGROUP**").

1.2. You accept this DPA when you accept the [Terms of Service](/terms). No separate signature is
needed.

1.3. If you need a signed copy for your records, write to `[legal contact email]` and we will send
one.

1.4. If this DPA and the Terms conflict on the protection of personal data, this DPA prevails.

## 2. Subject matter and duration

2.1. **Subject matter:** hosting the data you store in Cyphra Cloud databases, and the data you
send to them through the dashboard, the API or any Neo4j driver ("**Customer Data**").

2.2. **Duration:** for as long as FAKGROUP provides the Service to you, plus the period needed to
delete Customer Data under section 11.

## 3. Nature and purpose of the processing

3.1. **Nature:** storage, hosting, backup, restore, transmission, and the technical operations
needed to run the databases (starting, stopping, resizing, upgrading, monitoring).

3.2. **Purpose:** solely to provide the Service to you, as described in the Terms and as you
configure it.

3.3. FAKGROUP does not read, analyze, sell or use Customer Data for its own purposes. Staff look at
Customer Data only when you ask for support or when it is needed to keep the Service secure and
running.

## 4. Types of personal data and data subjects

4.1. You decide what Customer Data contains. FAKGROUP cannot see it in advance. It may include any
type of personal data, such as identification and contact details, account data, and the
relationships between people in your graph.

4.2. Data subjects are whoever your data is about, typically your end users, customers, employees
and contacts.

4.3. Do not store special categories of personal data (GDPR Article 9) or data about criminal
convictions (Article 10) unless you have assessed that the security measures in section 7 are
appropriate for them.

## 5. Your obligations

5.1. You are responsible for the lawfulness of the processing: the legal basis, the information
given to data subjects, and the accuracy of Customer Data.

5.2. Your instructions are these Terms and this DPA, and the settings you choose in the Service
(for example the region of a database). Other instructions must be in writing.

## 6. FAKGROUP's obligations

FAKGROUP will:

1. process Customer Data only on your documented instructions, including for transfers outside the
   EEA, unless EU or French law requires otherwise, in which case it will tell you first unless the
   law forbids it;
2. tell you if it believes an instruction breaks the GDPR;
3. make sure everyone authorized to process Customer Data is bound by confidentiality;
4. take the security measures in section 7;
5. follow the rules on sub-processors in section 8;
6. help you, as far as it can, to answer data subject requests (GDPR Articles 15 to 22), and pass
   on any request it receives directly, without answering it itself;
7. help you meet your obligations on security, breach notification, data protection impact
   assessments and prior consultation (GDPR Articles 32 to 36);
8. delete or return Customer Data at the end, under section 11;
9. give you the information you need to show compliance with Article 28, and allow audits under
   section 10.

## 7. Security measures

7.1. FAKGROUP takes technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

1. **Isolation:** each database runs as its own Neo4j instance, in a separate Kubernetes namespace
   per workspace, with a network policy and resource quota.
2. **Encryption in transit:** database endpoints use TLS (`neo4j+s://` for Bolt, HTTPS for the HTTP
   API) `[confirm: TLS enforced on every production cluster]`. The website, dashboard and API use
   HTTPS.
3. **Encryption at rest:** `[confirm: whether database volumes and backups are encrypted at rest, and how]`.
4. **Credentials:** a generated password per database. Database passwords are stored encrypted with
   AES-256-GCM. API keys and agent tokens are stored only as hashes.
5. **Backups:** on the plans that include them, a daily dump stored in S3-compatible object storage
   outside the database cluster, kept for the retention period of the plan
   `[confirm: backup storage provider, region, and that backups are enabled in production]`.
6. **Access control:** access to production systems is limited to authorized staff who need it
   `[confirm]`.
7. **Monitoring:** database status and storage use are monitored.

7.2. FAKGROUP may update these measures, as long as the overall level of security does not go down.

## 9. Personal data breaches

9.1. FAKGROUP will notify you **without undue delay, and at the latest within 72 hours**, after
becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Data.

9.2. The notice will describe, as far as known at the time: the nature of the breach, the
categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely
consequences, and the measures taken or proposed. Information that is not yet known will follow
as soon as it is.

9.3. FAKGROUP will help you notify the supervisory authority and the data subjects, where you need
to.

## 10. Audits

10.1. On request, FAKGROUP will provide the information reasonably needed to show that it complies
with this DPA, such as a description of its security measures and its sub-processors'
certifications.

10.2. If that is not enough, you may audit FAKGROUP, yourself or through an independent auditor
bound by confidentiality, once a year, with at least 30 days' notice, during business hours, and
without disrupting the Service or seeing other customers' data. You bear the costs of the audit,
unless it reveals a material breach of this DPA.

## 11. Deletion or return at the end

11.1. You can export Customer Data at any time while the Service runs, with any Neo4j tool or
driver.

11.2. When the Service ends, FAKGROUP keeps your databases and backups for 30 days, so you can
ask for access to export them. After that, it deletes all Customer Data, including backups, unless
EU or French law requires it to be kept.

11.3. When you delete a single database, it is deleted with its storage, and its backups are
deleted within 30 days `[confirm]`.

11.4. On request, FAKGROUP will confirm the deletion in writing.

## 12. International transfers

12.1. You choose the region of each database. FAKGROUP does not move a database to another region
without your instruction.

12.2. Where Customer Data is transferred outside the EEA (for example when you choose a US region,
or through a sub-processor in section 8), the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision (such as
the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for certified providers) or by the European Commission's
**Standard Contractual Clauses** (Decision (EU) 2021/914), Module 3 (processor to processor)
between FAKGROUP and the sub-processor, and Module 2 (controller to processor) between you and
FAKGROUP where needed. `[confirm: SCC modules with counsel]`
