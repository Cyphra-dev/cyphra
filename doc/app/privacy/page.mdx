(GDPR) and French law.

## 3. Cookies

3.1. We use only cookies that are **strictly necessary**: the session cookie that keeps you signed
in to the dashboard. Under the French Data Protection Act (art. 82) and CNIL guidance, these do not
need consent.

3.2. Our analytics (Vercel Web Analytics and Borromic) do not set cookies. We use no advertising or
tracking cookies.

3.3. Your theme choice (light or dark) is stored in your browser's local storage and never sent to
us.

3.4. If we add cookies that need consent, we will ask for it first and update this section.

## 5. International transfers

5.1. Some providers above are based in the United States or may access data from there. When
personal data leaves the European Economic Area, we rely on an adequacy decision (including the
EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for providers certified under it) or on the European Commission's
**Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)**, with extra safeguards where needed.

5.2. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards at [privacy@cyphra.dev](mailto:privacy@cyphra.dev).

## 6. Your rights

6.1. Under GDPR Articles 15 to 22, you have the right to:

1. **access** your personal data and get a copy (Art. 15);
2. have it **corrected** (Art. 16);
3. have it **erased** (Art. 17);
4. **restrict** its processing (Art. 18);
5. **receive it** in a portable format, or have it sent to another provider (Art. 20);
6. **object** to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21);
7. not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects
   you (Art. 22). We make no such decisions.

6.2. Under French law, you can also give instructions about what happens to your data after your
death.

6.3. To use any of these rights, write to [privacy@cyphra.dev](mailto:privacy@cyphra.dev). We answer within one month. We
may ask you to prove your identity.

6.4. You can complain to the French data protection authority, the **CNIL**
([www.cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, or to
the authority of the EU country where you live or work.

## 7. Security

7.1. We protect personal data with measures that match the risk, including:

1. HTTPS (TLS) for the website, the dashboard and the API;
2. passwords and API keys stored only as hashes;
3. database passwords and access tokens encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are stored;
4. access to production systems restricted to the people who need it `[confirm]`.

7.2. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we notify the CNIL within 72
hours and tell you without undue delay, as GDPR Articles 33 and 34 require.

## 8. Children
